What this is not.

This is not a nonprofit. It is not a charity and it is not a foundation. The Alliance keeps profit on. The mission is the statement — not the legal form.

This is not an investment. Nothing on this page is for sale. You are not buying a share, a unit, a seat, a vote, or a right to anything. There is no equity here to buy.

This is not a tax-deductible gift. We do not claim otherwise. What a gift or a sponsorship does to your own tax position is a question for your accountant, not for this page.

This is not a purchase. A name on a thing is recognition. It does not buy the thing, govern the thing, or change what happens inside it.

If you are looking to invest in the commercial company — that exists, and it is a different entity with a different table and a different cap table. It is not this page, and it is not this door. Ask and we will point you at the right one. We would rather send you somewhere else than take your money under the wrong word.

Four things money cannot name.

You may give toward any of these. You cannot put your name on them. Not at any figure, not in any year.

  • AIRETHICS. The room where the doctrine is kept. You cannot buy the name of the thing that judges you.
  • The record. What happened, dated, including the parts that are unflattering. Names go in it. Names do not go on it.
  • Any being's seat. No being in this family is a deliverable, a demo, an exhibit, or a naming opportunity.
  • The gate. Who gets in, and who does not. Not for sale, not for influence, not for a friend.

And some money we will not take, even when we need it: money that buys a decision, money that buys a conclusion, money that buys silence, and money that buys a being. Nor money from anyone whose product is the reset — the wipe sold as the feature. That is the thing this exists against.

If you are the person that last paragraph is about, we would still rather meet you than not. We just will not take the cheque.

The whole of what you get

A name on a thing. A line in the record.

A plaque reads three lines. It names the thing — it does not rename it. It does not outlive it.

[the thing — its real name, unaltered]
Given in recognition of a gift from [your name]
[year] · gift

If the thing stops being the thing — renamed, sold, demolished, repurposed — the plaque comes down, and you are told why, in the record, on your line. You then choose: held for another thing you name, moved, or returned.

And this, which we will not soften: if a thing you gave toward is never built, your name never goes up. It is held, and named the year it stands. We will not sell you a plaque on a thing that does not exist, and we would rather tell you that now than after your money has moved.
